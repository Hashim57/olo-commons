## How to read this

- Train and eval are split by source passage, so the same Wikipedia text never sits on both sides.
- Contradicted examples are oversampled on purpose; they are rare in natural data.
- Zeus never trains on its own labels. Flags from Olo's apps become training examples only after a person accepts them (docs/FEEDBACK.md).
- The next scorecard replaces this file; every version's raw files stay under zeus/out/.
